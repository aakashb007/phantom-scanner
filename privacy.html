Privacy Policy

Phantom Scanner · Effective 24 October 2026

Phantom Scanner ("the app") is a document scanner and PDF viewer for Android, built by Aakash Saleem. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. In short: your documents stay on your phone unless you choose to send them somewhere, the app has no ads or trackers, and the developer never receives your documents.

1. Data stored on your phone

Scanned pages, the text read from them, signatures, settings and recently opened files are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Scanning, text recognition (OCR), filters, translation of text and reading aloud run on your phone using Google ML Kit and the phone's own text-to-speech. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

2. Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to:

  • See your email address, only to show which account is connected.
  • Create and manage files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). The app can only see files it created; it cannot see or read any other files in your Drive.

Your documents are then saved as PDF files in a "Phantom Scanner" folder in your own Google Drive, and are kept in sync with the app. These files go directly between your phone and Google; they are not sent to the developer or anyone else. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and delete the files from your Google Drive whenever you like.

Phantom Scanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Ask AI search (optional)

If you add your own Groq API key and use Ask AI, your question and short excerpts from the documents that best match it are sent to Groq, Inc. to produce an answer. Documents in the Vault are never sent. Nothing is sent to Groq unless you ask a question. Groq's handling of that data is described in Groq's privacy policy.

4. Sharing

When you share or export a document (for example to WhatsApp or email), it goes to the app you choose. The app does not share documents on its own.

5. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • No selling or renting of any data.
  • No collection of your documents or personal data by the developer.

6. Security

Data on your phone is protected by Android's app storage. You can additionally turn on App Lock and keep documents in the fingerprint- or PIN-locked Vault. Connections to Google and Groq use encrypted HTTPS.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
