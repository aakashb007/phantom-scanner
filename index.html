Phantom Scanner

A private document scanner and PDF viewer for Android.

What it does

  • Scan documents with automatic edge detection, filters and Smart HD enhancement.
  • Read the text on scanned pages and search inside every document.
  • Open PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint files; compress, merge, split and password-protect PDFs.
  • Sign documents, add watermarks, and black out personal details before sharing.
  • Keep private documents in a fingerprint-locked Vault.
  • Optionally keep your documents in your own Google Drive.

Your privacy

Scanning and text reading happen on your phone. Documents only leave your phone when you choose to share them, sync them to your own Google Drive, or ask the optional AI search a question. There are no ads and no trackers. Read the full privacy policy.
